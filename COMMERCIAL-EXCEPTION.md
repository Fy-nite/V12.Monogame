# V12 Commercial Exception

This project is part of V12 and is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public
License, version 3.0 (`LICENCE` in this repository). **Version 1.0** of the V12
Commercial Exception applies to this copy.

## Short form

| Net revenue per calendar year | Fee | Closed source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| under $1,000 | $0 | no, the AGPL applies |
| $1,000 to $10,000 | $200 | yes |
| $10,000 to $100,000 | $500 | yes |
| over $100,000 | 1%, minimum $2,000 | yes |

- One license covers one studio and all of its titles.
- Tiers move up immediately when you cross a band, and are billed pro-rata from
  the day you cross.
- The paid tiers buy the right to distribute a V12-based product without
  publishing its source. They do not restrict any right you already have under
  the AGPL. If you would rather comply with the AGPL in full, you owe nothing at
  any revenue level.
